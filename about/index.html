About Halima

Nourishing families with premium dairy and juice products since 1998.

Company Introduction

A Trusted Name in Dairy & Juice

Halima is a leading Egyptian manufacturer of premium milk and fruit juice products, serving households, retailers, and hospitality partners across the nation. Built on decades of expertise, we combine traditional care with modern food science to deliver freshness families can rely on.

From our state-of-the-art facility in Cairo's industrial zone, we oversee every step — sourcing, processing, packaging, and distribution — ensuring that the Halima name stands for purity, nutrition, and consistent taste in every product.
